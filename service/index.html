Accommodation Types

Whitehorse accommodation falls into several categories. Downtown hotels offer walkability to restaurants, shops, and the waterfront. Airport hotels provide convenience for early flights. Vacation rentals give you a home kitchen and more space for families and groups. Wilderness lodges offer an immersive Yukon experience outside the city.

What's Included

  • Downtown hotels near restaurants and attractions
  • Airport hotels for early departures
  • Vacation rentals with full kitchens and laundry
  • Wilderness lodges with guided activities
  • Extended stay rates for long-term visitors
  • Pet-friendly options available
  • Seasonal availability and pricing guidance
