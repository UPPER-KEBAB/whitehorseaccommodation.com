Start with your priorities

Room to slow down. Space for your plans. Start with the small details that make a place feel right for your trip.

A base for exploring

Compare the location with your actual itinerary. Consider transport for early departures and late returns.

A place to settle in

Check cooking facilities, laundry, workspace and storage if you are staying longer or carrying outdoor gear.

A comfortable arrival

Confirm check-in arrangements, stairs or lift access, parking and how to get help outside reception hours.

Take the questions with you

Use the trip checklist to keep the important questions together. Confirm the answers directly with your chosen provider before making a commitment.
