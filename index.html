How do I compare the total cost?＋
Request a total for the same dates and party size, including taxes, cleaning and parking. Ask separately about any deposits. Keep refundable deposits separate from the stay price.
What should I check for a late arrival?＋
Ask how entry works after reception closes, how to reach someone if access fails and whether the arrival time must be agreed in advance.
