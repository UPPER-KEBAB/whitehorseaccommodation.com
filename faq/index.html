Common Questions

What is the best area to stay in Whitehorse?

Downtown Whitehorse puts you within walking distance of restaurants, the waterfront, and attractions. Airport area is convenient for early flights. Vacation rentals on the outskirts offer more space and quiet.

How much does a hotel in Whitehorse cost?

Summer rates (June-August) are highest, averaging $200-300 per night for standard hotels. Winter rates drop significantly. Vacation rentals vary by size and season.

Do I need to book in advance?

Summer (June-August) books up weeks in advance, especially for downtown hotels. Winter is quieter but aurora season (December-March) sees increased demand. Book early for peak periods.

Are there budget options?

Whitehorse has budget motels and hostels. Vacation rentals can be cost-effective for groups. Extended stay rates reduce per-night costs for longer visits.

Do hotels offer shuttle service?

Many Whitehorse hotels offer airport shuttle service. Contact the hotel directly or arrange airport transfer through our transport partners.

Can I bring my pet?

Some Whitehorse accommodation is pet-friendly. Vacation rentals often accept pets. Confirm before booking.
